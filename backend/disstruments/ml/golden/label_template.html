Golden set labeller

Label a real song you know well, by ear, with the specific instruments you hear. Taxonomy __TAXVER__. Nothing leaves your computer.
How to:
  1. Copy the song file into ~/datasets/golden/audio/ and pick it here.
  2. Mark each instrument you hear as present, or unsure if you can't tell (e.g. Rhodes vs Wurlitzer). Everything else counts as absent.
  3. Optional: while it plays, press ⏱ on a present instrument to mark when it starts, and again when it stops.
  4. Tick "listened to the whole song", then Save. Move the downloaded .labels.json into ~/datasets/golden/labels/, or tell Claude and it will collect it.
